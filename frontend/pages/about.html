About TraceVault

We help investigators trust what they find.

Fake screenshots, spoofed emails and edited documents are easy to make and hard to spot. TraceVault gives security teams and investigators a clear, evidence-based way to check digital files and explain the result.

Why we built TraceVault

Checking a suspicious file used to mean juggling separate tools: one for email headers, another for metadata, another for hashes, and a lot of notes in between.

TraceVault brings that work into one place. Upload an email, PDF, image, payment screenshot or link, and the platform examines it, collects the indicators it finds, and puts them in a report you can read in minutes.

We use AI to speed up the slow parts of an investigation. The judgment stays with you, and so does the evidence behind every result.

What guides our work

Three commitments shape every feature we build and every report we generate.

Evidence comes first

Every conclusion points back to something we found: a header, a hash, a metadata field or a link. If we can’t show it, we don’t claim it.

Speed without shortcuts

Automation handles the repetitive checks so you get results in minutes, while the full detail stays available when you want to dig deeper.

Reports people can use

Findings are written in plain language and organized so that colleagues, clients and decision-makers can follow them without being forensic experts.

Who TraceVault is for

Anyone who has to decide whether a digital file can be trusted, and show their reasoning.

  • Cybersecurity professionalsTriage phishing emails, suspicious attachments and unknown links.
  • Investigators and analystsExamine files, document integrity and build a clear record of what was found.
  • Teams that handle documentsCheck PDFs, invoices and payment screenshots before acting on them.

What TraceVault is, and isn’t

TraceVault is an AI-assisted analysis tool. It surfaces indicators and explains them, but it supports an investigation rather than replacing one. Treat results as evidence to review, not a final verdict.
